The Soul of Nepal

A Watch Is Small. The Story It Carries Is Not.

We did not set out to make watches. We set out to keep something alive — the courage of a soldier, the patience of a craftsman, the warmth of a stranger who calls you into their home. Nepal gives all of this freely. Every NEPAL timepiece is our way of carrying a little of it out into the world, and onto your wrist.

Diversity & Ethnicity

One Country, A Hundred Living Cultures

From the Sherpa of the high snow to the Newar craftsmen of the valley, the Tharu of the plains, the Gurung and Magar of the hills — over a hundred peoples share these mountains and more than a hundred tongues. Our dials borrow their patterns respectfully, each one named and explained, never decoration without meaning.

Nature & Inspiration

Eight of the World's Ten Highest Peaks

Glacier-fed rivers, rhododendron forests, terraced valleys, and skies so clear the stars feel close enough to touch. Nature here is not a backdrop — it is a teacher of patience and scale. We draw each dial from a real place, so that a single glance can return you to it.

Courage & Loyalty

Better to Die Than Live a Coward

For over two centuries the Gurkha has stood for a loyalty that asks nothing in return and a courage that never needs to raise its voice. We honour that spirit with restraint, not spectacle — a quiet field watch, built to be as dependable as the people who inspired it.

History & Craft

Hands That Have Carved for a Thousand Years

The temples of Kathmandu were raised by artisans whose skill passed father to child across generations. That same patience — the willingness to spend a lifetime perfecting one small thing — is the standard we hold every case, dial, and engraving to.

Generosity & Humanity

The Guest Is God — Atithi Devo Bhava

Trek any trail and a family with little will still share their tea, their fire, their story. That open-handed warmth is Nepal's true wealth. A portion of every sale returns to the mountain communities and craftspeople who make this heritage possible.

Love & Emotion

Some Things Are Meant to Be Handed Down

A watch marks time, but it also holds it — the day, the name, the words you chose to engrave on the back. We build these to outlast us, so that one day they can be given again, carrying not just the hour but the whole of a feeling.

"You are not only buying a watch. You are carrying a piece of Nepal — its mountains, its people, and the quiet, stubborn love they have for this land."
